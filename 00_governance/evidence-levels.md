# Evidence Levels

- **VERIFIED** — directly observed in an ERP page, field, message, export or verified case.
- **SOP-CONFIRMED** — supported by SOP, explicit process or user-directed rule.
- **INFERRED** — plausible but not sufficiently verified as a general rule.
- **UNKNOWN** — evidence is insufficient.

Do not let historical cases override active rules.
